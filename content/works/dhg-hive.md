## The community the software came from

DHG found me at nineteen and gave me a place where I could lead as myself. I carried that gift forward by helping other people feel seen, capable, and worth fighting for.

June 12, 2013 is the date on the home page for the night a stranger named Saturn spoke to me in a Call of Duty lobby and invited me into DHG. I said yes. That answer gave me people who had my back, a place to lead, and proof that the person I had hidden could still be wanted.

The learning page records the community lesson beside that belonging. At nineteen, an invitation through a headset gave me belonging at the exact moment I needed it. Leadership later gave me a way to pass that gift forward. I built training, schedules, dashboards, and leadership paths that helped members see their ability, support each other, and carry the community forward.

The home page also records that I helped grow a 46-member squad to 85 percent activity. I trained members after difficult rounds, built the dashboards and schedules, and developed officers who could carry the work themselves.
